#4471 says every report about a wrong date is one bug: the parser reads dates month first. Reading the reports by tenant, channel and what each one says shows three problems with three different fixes. Each now has its own ticket.

Fixing the parser closes only the first row. The other two stay open until their own fixes ship.

  1. The parser reads month first (#1). Every tenant on file writes the day first. A date with a day of 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. It reaches the desk through every channel.
  2. The booking-v3 hint says MM/DD/YYYY (#2). The form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first, but tells people to type the month first. They do as told, and the date swaps. Same symptom as the parser, different cause: only reports sent through a booking-v3 form, and the parser fix does not touch it.
  3. The date moves by one day (#3). Tuesday shows as Monday; a meeting goes out as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking. That is time-zone handling, not a swap. The desk had already noted these reports as read differently (desk-notes.json), but filed them under the parser by tenant.

Why they were merged

The desk merges on subject alone (decision 0001), and nobody reads the body. People with all three problems write the same subjects: “Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”. The cause depends on the tenant (its locale and booking form), the channel, and what the body describes; intake’s tag is a guess from the browser and often disagrees.
